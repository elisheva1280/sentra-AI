Sentra AI Mobile App

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is collected, used, shared, and protected when customers use the Sentra AI mobile app for camera alert and security-system management.

Last Updated: October 11, 2026. This Privacy Policy describes how Sentra AI handles information when you use the mobile app. For account deletion instructions, see the Account Deletion Request page.

Introduction

Sentra AI provides a customer-facing mobile application for managing connected NVR and camera alert systems. The app lets customers sign in with an email address or a mobile phone number, view connected NVR cameras, manage linked cameras, configure camera alert behavior, set mute schedules, choose detection classes and mask areas, view alert information, verify a WhatsApp number for alert notifications, and get help from an AI support assistant in the app.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sentra AI mobile app and the customer-facing services that support it. It does not cover separate admin, operator, or internal tools except where those systems are used to provide or support the mobile app service.

Image Processing During NVR Setup

When you choose to add an NVR from an image, the app asks for permission to use your device camera or photo library. The image you select is sent to Sentra AI servers and passed to the Google Gemini API solely to identify the connection details it contains.

  • What is collected: the image itself, and the connection details extracted from it — IP address, port, NVR username, and NVR password.
  • Why: to save you from typing the connection details manually.
  • Third party: we use the Google Gemini API. Under Google's paid services terms, the images are not used to train Google's models. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Storage: the image is stored in our AWS S3 storage.

This feature is optional. You can always enter NVR connection details manually instead, and the app does not access your camera or photo library unless you start this flow.

AI Support Chat

The app includes an optional support chat with an AI assistant (Alex). When you use it, your messages are sent to Sentra AI servers and passed to the Google Gemini API to generate replies.

  • What is processed: the messages you write, the history of the current conversation (sent with each message), and information from your account that is relevant to your request — for example NVR, camera, connection-status, alert, and statistics data.
  • Why: to answer your questions, help you troubleshoot, and carry out actions you ask for in your account. A record of actions the assistant performs (action type, status, and a summary) is kept with your account records.
  • Third party: we use the Google Gemini API. Under Google's paid services terms, this data is not used to train Google's models. We take steps to mask device passwords and similar secrets in account data before it is sent to the model. Text you type is sent as written, so please do not type passwords or other sensitive information that is not needed. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Storage: your past conversations (up to the 20 most recent) are saved on your device so you can reopen them; our servers do not keep a chat history. Short-lived conversation and task state is kept on our servers for a limited time (from about 15 minutes up to 24 hours, depending on the task) so that multi-step requests can be completed.

  • NVR and camera integration services: camera and NVR connection details are processed by backend services that connect to your NVR device to retrieve device status, channel lists, snapshots, and alert data.

If you use the in-app AI support assistant, your messages and related account information are sent to the Google Gemini API to generate replies (see AI Support Chat). PostHog data is processed in PostHog's EU cloud region. Google and PostHog process this data as our service providers. We may use additional or alternative analytics providers in the future, under equivalent terms. See the Firebase Privacy and Security information, the Google Privacy Policy, and the PostHog Privacy Policy.

Data may be processed in countries where these service providers operate. Where applicable laws require safeguards for international data transfers, we rely on contractual and organizational measures consistent with those requirements.

Analytics and Your Choices

We use analytics and app-performance services, currently Google Firebase Analytics and PostHog, to understand in aggregate how the app is used, which features are used, and where errors or performance problems occur. Analytics is used for product improvement, reliability, and security only.

As the app evolves, we may add, change, or remove the specific events and measurements we collect, and we may use additional or alternative analytics providers. Any such changes remain subject to the commitments below.

  • Product improvement only: we use analytics to improve, secure, and troubleshoot the app — not to make decisions about individual users.
  • No advertising or tracking: we do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking. Analytics data is not sold or shared with advertising networks or data brokers.
  • No sensitive content: we do not send camera images, snapshots, video, alert media, NVR credentials, or WhatsApp message content to analytics providers.
  • Retention: analytics event data is retained according to the retention period configured with our analytics provider, after which it is deleted or retained only in aggregated form.
  • Your choices: you can turn analytics off at any time in the app under Settings → Share usage data; this stops collection by both Firebase Analytics and PostHog on that device. You can also limit analytics collection through your device operating system privacy settings, or by uninstalling the app. You may also contact us at support@sentraai.ai to request that analytics data associated with your app installation be deleted.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

  • Data in transit: communication between the mobile app and service APIs is protected using HTTPS (TLS).
  • Authentication: authenticated access controls are used to protect account and service access.
  • Session protection: session information is stored using secure platform capabilities available on the device.
  • NVR credentials: device connection details, including NVR usernames and passwords, IP addresses and ports, are stored on our servers so the service can connect to your equipment. Access is restricted to the systems and personnel required to operate the service.
  • Local alert cache: recent alert notifications, including their titles and text, are cached in app storage on your device so you can view them offline. This cache is limited in size, is excluded from device cloud backup on Android, and is cleared when you sign out or uninstall the app.

Local Storage (Archive)

The app lets you save alerts, images, and videos to your device's local storage. These items are stored only on your device, are not sent to our servers, and are not accessible to us. They remain until you delete them. You can also save images and videos to your device gallery — this happens only at your initiative and requires your permission.

  • NVR and camera configuration and credentials may be retained as needed to connect and operate supported devices and to support service and security functions, depending on configuration and operational requirements.

  • Support and deletion requests may be retained as needed to process requests, provide support, and maintain required records.

We retain data for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.
